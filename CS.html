About Computer Science

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science

The Department of Computer Science at Government College Women University, Sialkot, offers learning opportunities in computing, programming, information technology, and related areas.

This website brings together program information, the admission criteria supplied for this student project, faculty information, and official university links in one place.

Official Department Page

Our Vision

To help students develop the academic, technical, and professional skills needed to contribute to computing and society.

Our Focus

Programming, software development, data, networks, research, and responsible use of technology.

Admission Information

BS CS & BS IT — Eligibility

  • FSc Pre-Medical, FSc Pre-Engineering, or ICS.
  • Minimum marks: 65%, according to the criteria supplied for this project.
  • FA is not accepted under the criteria supplied for this project.
  • Morning and Evening (SSDP) options are listed for BS programs.

Admission Semesters

  • BS Computer Science: Spring.
  • BS Information Technology: Fall and Spring.
  • MS CS / MS IT: Confirm current schedule and availability through official notices.
  • Admission dates and merit depend on the university's official announcement.
Important: These BS criteria reflect the details provided by the website owner, not a verification of current official policy. The university's published prospectus may state different requirements. Please verify the latest official prospectus and admission advertisement before relying on these criteria.
